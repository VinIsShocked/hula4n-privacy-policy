Privacy Policy

HULA4N MO ANG LARAWAN

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HULA4N MO ANG LARAWAN (“the App”) handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

The App is a Filipino picture-word puzzle game. Based on its current implementation, the developer does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other directly identifying information to play the game.

The App may store game progress and in-game coin balances locally on your device. This information is used to support gameplay and is not intended to identify you.

2. Third-Party Services

The App may be distributed through Google Play. Google Play and your device platform may process information under their own privacy policies when you download, update, or use the App.

As of the effective date above, this policy assumes the App does not include advertising, analytics, account-login, or cloud-storage SDKs. If such services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe the services and their data practices.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay information is used to maintain game progress and features such as in-game coins. The developer does not sell personal information collected through the App.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer through the App, please contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

5. Data Security and Retention

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device unless it is removed by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App. The developer does not operate a game account system or server-based save system under the assumptions described in this policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same webpage address.

7. Contact

For privacy questions about HULA4N MO ANG LARAWAN, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the App’s Google Play store listing.
